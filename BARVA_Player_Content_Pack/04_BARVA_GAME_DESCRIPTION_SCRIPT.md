# BARVA — Game Description Script

**Target length:** 90–120 seconds  
**Purpose:** Website video, store-page feature video, prototype introduction or developer showcase  
**Tone:** Clear, confident and atmospheric rather than overhyped

---

## Opening

**VISUAL:** Slow shots of the pub before opening: empty bar, pool table, stock shelves, early light.

**VOICEOVER:**

> BARVA is a systems-driven pub management game about everything that happens behind the pint.

> You take control of a struggling local and guide it night by night—managing staff, stock, prices, security, reputation and the constant pressure of keeping the business alive.

---

## The Nightly Loop

**VISUAL:** Preparation menus, staff and stock checks, opening the pub, customers entering.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Before opening, you prepare the venue.  
> You order supplies, organise the team, set prices, choose a security policy and decide whether tonight is a normal service—or something bigger.

> Once the doors open, the night unfolds in rounds.

> Customers arrive with different budgets, expectations and levels of trouble. Your staff must serve them before frustration becomes chaos, while you react to incidents and use limited Landlord Actions to steady the room.

---

## Cause and Effect

**VISUAL:** Show a clean service, then contrast it with overload: unserved customers, refunds, fights and staff stress.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Nothing in BARVA exists alone.

> Poor staffing increases workload.  
> High workload damages service.  
> Bad service creates refunds and angry customers.  
> Chaos hurts morale.  
> Low morale makes the next night even harder.

> But strong decisions compound as well.

> A dependable team creates better service. Better service builds reputation. Reputation attracts better customers, supports stronger prices and gives the pub room to grow.

---

## Weekly Strategy

**VISUAL:** Night report transitions into Payday, bills, upgrades and milestone unlocks.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Every seven nights, Payday arrives.

> Wages, rent, supplier invoices and debt all come due. A busy pub is not automatically a healthy business, and spending every pound on expansion can leave you unable to survive the week.

> Reports reveal the patterns behind each result, helping you identify whether the real problem is stock, staffing, morale, security, pricing—or the strategy itself.

---

## Build Your Pub

**VISUAL:** Activities, different crowd types, upgraded interiors, VIPs, security, inn rooms, rival updates.

**VOICEOVER:**

> As the pub develops, you unlock new activities, upgrades and opportunities.

> Run quiz nights, karaoke, live acoustic sets, DJ nights, pool nights, darts competitions, family lunches or tasting menus. Build relationships with named regulars. Develop a reputation as a respectable local, an artsy venue, a family-friendly pub, an underground haunt, a rowdy favourite—or something shady.

> Later, expand into lodging, respond to seasonal demand and face pressure from rival venues across the district.

---

## Close

**VISUAL:** A full pub operating smoothly, then cut to a more chaotic alternative. Exterior night shot.

**VOICEOVER:**

> BARVA is not about finding one perfect build.

> It is about understanding a living system, making difficult trade-offs and building a venue that can survive the consequences of your decisions.

> Run the night. Pay the bills. Build the story.

> Welcome to BARVA.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

> **BARVA**  
> **A SYSTEMS-DRIVEN PUB MANAGEMENT SIMULATION**

---

## Short Store Description

Run a living British pub where every decision travels through the business. Manage staff, stock, prices, security, activities, reputation and weekly bills as you turn a struggling local into a respected institution—or a legendary disaster. In BARVA, a packed house is only a success if you can survive the consequences.

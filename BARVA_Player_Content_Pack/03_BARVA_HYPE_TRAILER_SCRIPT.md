# BARVA — Hype Trailer Script

**Target length:** 60–75 seconds  
**Tone:** Cinematic, sharp, tense, darkly playful  
**Delivery:** Start controlled; build speed and intensity toward the title reveal

---

## 0:00–0:06 — The Doors

**VISUAL:** Empty pub. Morning light. Chairs still. A key turns in the front door.  
**SFX:** Lock clicks. Low room tone. A distant glass placed on wood.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Every pub looks peaceful before the doors open.

---

## 0:07–0:16 — First Customers

**VISUAL:** Lights come on. Staff take positions. First punters enter. Till opens. Drinks begin moving.  
**MUSIC:** A restrained beat begins.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Then the stock starts moving.  
> The orders start stacking.  
> And every empty seat becomes a promise you have to keep.

---

## 0:17–0:28 — Pressure Builds

**VISUAL:** Faster cuts—crowd growing, staff working, stock falling, cash rising, one customer waiting, chaos meter creeping upward.  
**SFX:** Orders, glasses, crowd noise layered into the beat.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Set the prices.  
> Hire the team.  
> Choose the music.  
> Work the crowd.  
> Pay the wages.  
> Control the door.

---

## 0:29–0:40 — Consequences

**VISUAL:** Unserved customer becomes rowdy. A fight begins. Security intervenes. A Trading Standards warning appears. A rumour spreads. Staff morale drops.  
**MUSIC:** Beat distorts; tension rises.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Because one slow round becomes an angry crowd.  
> One missed wage becomes a staff crisis.  
> And one bad week can turn the whole town against you.

---

## 0:41–0:52 — The Choice

**VISUAL:** Split rhythm between a calm, respected pub and a loud, chaotic packed house. Quiz night. Live band. VIP regular. Upgrade installed. Payday screen.  
**MUSIC:** Opens into a larger, confident theme.

**VOICEOVER:**

> Build a respected local.  
> Create an underground institution.  
> Run the safest house in town…  
> or make chaos your business model.

---

## 0:53–1:03 — The System

**VISUAL:** Rapid montage of reports, activities, staff, security, rivals, rooms, upgrades and the pub changing through different times of day.  
**VOICEOVER:**

> Every customer has a limit.  
> Every decision has a cost.  
> Every night becomes part of your story.

---

## 1:04–1:12 — Title Reveal

**VISUAL:** Exterior at night. Neon and pub light against darkness. Cut to black. BARVA title appears.  
**MUSIC:** Final impact, then room ambience.

**VOICEOVER:**

> The doors are open.  
> What kind of landlord will you become?

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

> **BARVA**  
> **RUN THE NIGHT. OWN THE CHAOS.**

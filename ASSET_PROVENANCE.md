# BARVA Asset Provenance

Gnosis Interactive has confirmed that it owns or has distribution permission
for all music, sound recordings, images, and depicted-person content included
in BARVA v3.1.0, including distribution through GitHub and itch.io.

## Artwork

BARVA's original in-game artwork and background artwork were created by Gnosis
Interactive without generative AI.

The loading-sequence polaroids are AI-generated privacy-protecting images.
They are intentionally distinct from the original in-game artwork and
background artwork.

## Music and sound recordings

The included music is described by Gnosis Interactive as licence-free. Gnosis
Interactive has confirmed that it has the necessary rights or permission to
distribute all included music and sound recordings with BARVA.

## Images and depicted people

Gnosis Interactive has confirmed that it owns or has distribution permission
for all included images and any depicted-person content.
